linkedin-outreach|Start a professional networking conversation|Use a relevant professional context to open a conversation without a disguised pitch.|Check that the role and account fit your offer.~Identify a public professional topic worth discussing.~Write a message that stands on its own without exaggerated familiarity.~Ask a small question before proposing a meeting.|a professional connection message|relevant conversations rather than connection acceptance alone|A connection acceptance does not mean consent to a long sales sequence. referral-request|Ask for a relevant introduction|Make an introduction easy to evaluate and easy to decline.|Explain the type of team you can responsibly help.~Give a specific reason the introduction could be useful.~Provide a short forwardable description.~Let the connector decide whether and how to introduce you.|a forwardable introduction note|introductions that reach a suitable business conversation|Do not ask a contact to vouch for results they have not seen.
